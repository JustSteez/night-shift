Three shifts.
One vault.

  1. 9:30 — 4:00 ET

    Day shift

    The NYSE is open and prices are live. LPs deposit USDG at NAV and get nsUSDG shares. The desk is closed.

  2. 4:00 — 9:30 ET, weekends, holidays

    Night shift

    The desk opens. Anyone can buy or sell stock tokens at the last Chainlink close ± spread. When pools drift, arbitrageurs trade with the desk and the spread goes to LPs.

  3. 24 / 7

    Clock out

    Burn your shares any time for a pro-rata slice of everything in the vault: USDG plus stock tokens. No oracle needed.

Weekend news gap

The desk quotes Friday’s close. If big news breaks on Saturday, traders can pick off the vault before Monday. Spreads, pauses and caps limit it. LPs carry it.
